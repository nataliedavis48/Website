The word Geisha actually tells us exactly who they are. In Japanese, "Gei" means art and "sha" means person. So, a Geisha is literally an "art person" or an artist.

Sam: That makes sense. They are professional entertainers who spend years practicing traditional Japanese arts. They are experts in classical music, dance, and the famous tea ceremony. I read that they have to study for many years before they can even call themselves a Geisha.

Alex: Exactly. It is a very long and difficult journey. In Kyoto, which is the most famous city for this culture, a student is called a Maiko. A Maiko is an apprentice. They usually start their training as teenagers. They live in a special house called an okiya, and they learn everything from how to walk gracefully to how to speak politely to guests.

Sam: I have seen pictures of them. The Maiko usually wear very colorful kimonos and have many decorations in their hair. Their makeup is also very white. Why is the makeup so white, Alex?

Alex: Well, in the past, there was no electricity. They used candles to light the rooms. The white makeup helped their faces stand out and look beautiful in the soft candlelight. It is a tradition that they still keep today, even though we have bright electric lights now.

Sam: That is fascinating. So, what happens when they finish their training?

Alex: After about five years of being a Maiko, they become a Geiko. Geiko is the word used in Kyoto for a fully qualified Geisha. Their appearance changes a bit. Their kimonos become more simple and elegant, and they wear wigs instead of styling their natural hair every day.

Sam: And what is their role in Japanese culture today? Are they still important?

Alex: Very much so. They are like living museums. Their main role is to entertain guests at high-end restaurants or tea houses. They make the guests feel comfortable, they pour drinks, and they perform beautiful dances or play instruments like the shamisen, which is a three-stringed guitar.

Sam: It sounds like a very expensive experience. I imagine not everyone can just go and meet a Geisha.

Alex: You are right. It is quite private and very expensive. Usually, you need an introduction from a regular customer to visit a tea house where Geishas work. However, nowadays, some places offer performances for tourists so more people can see their art.

Sam: It is amazing how they preserve these ancient traditions in such a modern country like Japan. They represent the history and the grace of the past.
